Accessibility

Roles, ARIA wiring and focus management come from @radix-ui/react-label. Do not re-implement them.

Rolelabel
KeyboardClicking the label focuses or toggles the associated control
RequiredhtmlFor matching the control id
  • A label without htmlFor is decorative text and provides no accessible name.
  • Radix Label prevents text selection on double-click, which stops accidental selection when toggling a checkbox.
