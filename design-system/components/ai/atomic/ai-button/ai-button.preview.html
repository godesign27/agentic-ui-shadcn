AIAction composition

AIAction ships alongside AIButton — primary + secondary + tertiary row with optional review pill.

Documented states

  • Primary — brand gradient CTA with action shadow; status spinner/check/error
  • Secondary — Standard border outline, AI tint on hover
  • Tertiary — ghost helper text, darkens on hover
  • Loading / complete — click suppressed; minWidth prevents reflow
  • Disabled — reduced opacity across all variants
